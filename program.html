Our 4-Week Program

Code Blinks — 4-Week Program

Our four-week program introduces students to the fundamentals of coding and artificial intelligence while creating space for conversations around mental well-being, healthy habits, and personal growth.

WEEK 1

Introduction to Coding

Start your journey into technology by understanding coding, programming, algorithms, problem solving, and the basics of artificial intelligence.

  • What Is Coding?
  • How Programming Works
  • Programming Languages
  • Algorithms & Problem Solving
  • Introduction to AI
WEEK 2

Coding Fundamentals

Build a foundation in programming concepts and learn how to approach problems through logical thinking and experimentation.

  • Variables & Data Types
  • Conditions & Logic
  • Loops
  • Functions
  • Debugging Basics
WEEK 3

Mental Well-Being & Awareness

Explore the importance of mental well-being, understand everyday pressures, and learn healthier ways to manage stress and support personal growth.

  • Understanding Mental Health
  • Mental Health Awareness
  • Managing Stress
  • Building Healthy Habits
  • Self-Awareness & Emotional Well-Being
WEEK 4

Technology, Mind & Future

Bring technology and personal well-being together while thinking about responsible technology use, artificial intelligence, and the future.

  • Technology & Mental Well-Being
  • Responsible Use of Technology
  • AI & the Future
  • Building a Positive Digital Life
  • Final Project & Reflection
