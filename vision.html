Our direction

Vision & Overview

Building young creators who can grow technically and personally in a supportive learning environment.

Vision

To empower a generation of confident, capable, and mentally resilient young creators who use technology to learn, innovate, and build a better future together.

Code Blinks envisions a future where students have access not only to opportunities for learning technology, but also to the awareness, support, and environment they need to grow as individuals.

We believe that young people should not have to sacrifice their well-being in the pursuit of academic or personal success. By combining technology education with conversations around mental well-being, we aim to help students develop both the skills to build their future and the awareness to take care of themselves along the way.

Who we are

Overview

Code Blinks is a global, student-led online community where students learn, connect, and grow through technology and shared experiences.

We introduce students to the fundamentals of coding, artificial intelligence, and the real-world applications of AI through interactive classes, workshops, and collaborative learning.

As part of our initial learning initiative, we conducted a 4-week online program, with sessions held once or twice a week. Alongside introducing students to basic coding concepts, the sessions also created space to discuss mental well-being and healthy ways of dealing with pressure.

More than technical education

Our mission extends beyond technical education. We recognize that students today can experience significant pressure from academics, expectations, relationships, personal challenges, and other aspects of life at a very young age.

Code Blinks aims to encourage young people to recognize the importance of their mental well-being, talk about their experiences, and develop healthier approaches to handling everyday challenges.
