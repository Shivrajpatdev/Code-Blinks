A Note from the Founder

I’m the Founder & CEO of Code Blinks, where I focus on helping students learn the basics of coding while creating awareness about the importance of mental health and mental well-being.

Through Code Blinks, I aim to make technology easier and more approachable for students, while also encouraging open conversations about mental health. I believe that learning, creativity, and personal well-being should grow together.

My goal is to build a community where students can learn, connect, support one another, and grow with confidence.
