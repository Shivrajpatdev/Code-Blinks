What we aim to create

Our Impact

Through Code Blinks, our impact focuses on two connected areas: building digital skills and protecting young people's well-being.

💻

Technology & Digital Learning

  • Introduced students to the fundamentals of coding in an accessible and beginner-friendly way.
  • Encouraged students to explore AI and its applications in everyday life.
  • Created opportunities to learn through interactive classes and collaborative activities.
  • Encouraged a mindset of creating and experimenting with technology, rather than simply consuming it.
🧠

Mental Well-being

  • Raised awareness about the importance of mental health among young people.
  • Opened conversations around academic and personal pressures students may experience.
  • Encouraged students to recognize when they are overwhelmed and prioritize healthy habits, self-awareness, and appropriate support.
  • Promoted personal well-being as an essential part of growth and success.
🌍

Community & Growth

  • Created a space where students can learn, connect, share ideas, and grow together.
  • Promoted an inclusive learning environment where beginners can participate without feeling intimidated.
  • Connected technology learning with confidence, creativity, collaboration, and personal growth.
